# Kurník Šopa

A small regenerative farm in Křepice u Hustopečí selling eggs, pasture-raised poultry,
rabbits and vegetables directly to customers, and publishing news about the farm.
This glossary is the shared vocabulary for the storefront, the admin and the tickets.

## Language

### People

**Customer**:
A person who orders from the farm, with or without a User account.
_Avoid_: buyer, client, account

**Guest Order**:
An Order placed by a Customer without a User account. It is reached through the
tokenised link in its confirmation email, never by its order number alone.
_Avoid_: anonymous order, guest checkout (that is the act, this is the result)

**Access Request**:
A visitor's request for a Customer account, waiting for the owner to turn it into an
Invitation.
_Avoid_: sign-up, registration, application

**Invitation**:
The owner's act of granting a person a User account. There is no open sign-up; ordering
needs no account.
_Avoid_: registration, sign-up, approval

**User**:
A login account with one role: `admin`, `staff`, `editor` or `customer`.
_Avoid_: account, member

**Staff**:
A User who handles orders and pickups for the farm.
_Avoid_: operator, farm admin

**Editor**:
A User who writes and publishes blog posts and nothing else.
_Avoid_: author (an Author is a byline, not a role)

### Selling

**Order**:
A Customer's commitment to take a quantity of one or more products from the farm,
paid in cash at pickup. An Order against a Batch starts as a Booking.
_Avoid_: purchase, transaction, reservation

**Booking**:
An Order placed against a Batch whose pickup dates are not yet confirmed. It becomes a
confirmed Order when the Customer confirms it after the dates are announced, or is
released if they do not.
_Avoid_: reservation, pre-order, waiting list

**Pickup Point**:
A place where Customers collect their Orders: the farm itself or another location
the owner designates.
_Avoid_: delivery address, branch, depot

**Roster**:
The per-Batch list Staff use on pickup day: who collects what, where, the weight
recorded and the cash taken.
_Avoid_: packing list, manifest, export

**Batch**:
A period in which one product is available for pickup, such as a single run of chickens
("turnus" in Czech). Customers can book against a Batch before its pickup dates are known.
_Avoid_: run, season, cycle, period

**Payment**:
Cash handed over at pickup. The site itself never takes money.
_Avoid_: online payment, deposit, card payment, bank transfer
